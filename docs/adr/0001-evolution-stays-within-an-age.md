# Evolution stays within an Age

A Unit evolves by merging duplicates (SAP-style levelling), which moves it through three Stages that each have their own name and look. A Unit never crosses into a later Age. Modernizing means selling an old Unit and buying a newer one. We considered cross-Age evolution (a Stone Unit turning into its Bronze form, possibly keeping its investment or old ability), but it added too many interacting dimensions (Families, Forms, evolution costs, level-versus-evolve choices) for a simple SAP-like game.

## Considered Options

- Evolution into the next Age's form as a paid action, a shop item, or a choice at level-up. All of these were rejected as too complex for v1. Cross-Age evolution can return later as a specific Unit ability rather than a universal rule.
