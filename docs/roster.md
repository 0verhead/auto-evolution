# v1 Roster

Three Units per Age (Front, Back, Bridge). Stats are borrowed from a Counterpart in the SAP Turtle Pack tier that arrives in the same Round. Ability numbers are listed for Stage 1 / 2 / 3. Names are placeholders.

## Inventions

Each Invention borrows the effect of a SAP Turtle Pack food from the matching tier (checked against the wiki). Perk foods are left out because v1 has no held items. Each costs 3 gold.

| Age | Invention | Art | SAP food | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stone | Fire | 🔥 | Apple | Give a Unit +1/+1. |
| Bronze | Beer | 🍺 | Cupcake | Give a Unit +3/+3 until end of battle. |
| Iron | Roads | 🛣️ | Salad Bowl | Give 2 random Units +1/+1. |
| Modern | Canned Food | 🥫 | Canned Food | Give all current and future shop Units +1/+1. |
| Sci-Fi | Gene Editing | 🧬 | Chocolate | Give a Unit +1 experience. |
